__Mastodon__ is a decentralized microblogging service designed as a decentralized network that is not limited to a single platform.

---

The Mastodon instance [social.muenchen.de](https://social.muenchen.de) provides an official communication platform for the departments, municipal enterprises, and associated companies of the City of Munich.
