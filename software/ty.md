__ty__ is an extremely fast type checker and language server for [Python](python), written in Rust. It comes from the makers of [Ruff](ruff) and [uv](uv) and is meant as a modern, high-performance alternative to mypy.

---

Adopting ty early fits our toolchain: it integrates tightly with Ruff and uv, is used across all of our [Python](python)-based AI systems — [MUCGPT](mucgpt), [Zammad-AI](zammad-ai), [RIS-KI](riski) and the Dienstleistungsfinder KI-Suche — and supports gradual typing, so existing code bases can be checked section by section.
