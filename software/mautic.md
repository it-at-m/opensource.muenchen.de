Mautic is open-source marketing automation software.

---

The City of Munich (LHM) uses Mautic to personalize our web content in [Magnolia](magnolia)—for example, on [stadt.muenchen.de](https://stadt.muenchen.de/)—for specific user groups and to provide them with relevant, personalized content.
