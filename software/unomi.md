Apache __Unomi__ is an open-source customer data platform for managing customer, lead, and visitor data.

---

The City of Munich uses Unomi to personalize our web content in [Magnolia](magnolia)—for example, on [stadt.muenchen.de](https://stadt.muenchen.de/)—for specific user groups and to offer them personalized content tailored to their needs.
